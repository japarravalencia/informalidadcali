Interactive Policy Sandbox: Reactive Welfare, Defensive Informality, and Viable Municipal Governance

Between 2020 and 2024, official extreme monetary poverty in Santiago de Cali fell from 13.3% to 6.1%, yet labor informality remained rigidly entrenched above 45–51%. Explore the calibrated continuous-time ODE model behind our Systems (MDPI) study to test how municipal welfare governance, punitive Sisbén/RUI audits, youth capability ring-fencing, and multidimensional metrics reshape Cali's socioeconomic trajectory through 2040.

3. Complete Model Documentation & User Guide

Full open-science documentation of the VSM cybernetic diagnosis, continuous-time ODE equations, calibrated parameters, Stage 5 validation tests, and guided policy exploration exercises.

Pathology 1 · S3–S4 Homeostat

Short-Termism & Capability Starvation

Electoral pressure for immediate poverty relief causes System 3 operational transfers (Greac) to crowd out System 4 youth capability budgets (INNAJ), starving an 8-year maturation pipeline (τform = 7.64 yr) whenever monetary indicators improve (Loop B2).

Pathology 2 · System 3* Channel

Punitive Audit Hypertrophy

Turning the sporadic System 3* audit channel into permanent punitive surveillance (Afisc) to purge Sisbén/RUI rosters violates Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety: System 1 workers counter institutional control by hiding assets and rejecting formal payroll contracts (Loop R2).

Pathology 3 · System 5 Identity

Corrupted Algedonic Signal

Anchoring System 5 policy success solely to headline extreme monetary poverty (Pmon,rep) blinds municipal leadership: saturating cash transfers keep Pmon near 6.6–7.0% even when informality surges to 69.8%.

Operational Unit Definition (1 CIU): Youth human capital CH(t) is measured in Capability Index Units (CIU), where 1.00 CIU denotes a composite municipal cohort-readiness benchmark combining secondary/technical completion, early-childhood nutrition, and formal employability (anchored from 0.52 CIU in 2010 to 0.980 CIU in 2026). The labor-market threshold is Creq,lab = 0.70 CIU, while the unclamped indicator frontier is Cref,ind = 2.00 CIU.
1. Endogenous Policy Indices [Eqs. (1)–(4)]
V_hogar(t) = 0.55 * I_rate(t) + 0.45 * IPM(t)
MPI_Adj(t) = clamp(0.40 + 0.60*V_hogar - 0.40*min(1, C_H/2.0), 0.02, 0.98)
ICE(t) = clamp(1 - I_rate * (1 - 0.50*min(1, C_H/2.0)), 0, 1)
IT(t) = I_rate(t) / P_mon(t)

Dimensionless macro-indicators capturing structural deprivation, decent work quality, and indicator decoupling.

2. Human Capital Accumulation [Eq. (5)]
dC_H/dt = DELAY3((I_NNAJ(t) / Pi(t)) * eta_cap, tau_form)
         - C_H(t) / tau_vida

Third-order Erlang delay (τform = 7.64 yr; τvida = 25 yr). Both input and output of DELAY3 have units of CIU/yr; nominal investment is deflated by Π(t) = (1 + 0.0499)t-2010.

3. Reactive Informality & Grace Period [Eqs. (6)–(9)]
dP_inf/dt = F_prec(t) - F_form(t)
F_form = alpha_0 * P_inf * min(1.8, C_H/0.70) * (1 - mu_sisben*phi)
F_prec = beta_0 * (L_urb - P_inf) * max(0.5, 1.5 - C_H/0.70)
         * (1 + sigma * A_fisc * mu_sisben + lambda_cov * K_covid)
mu_sisben(T_grace) = mu_floor + (0.45 - mu_floor) * exp(-0.12 * T_grace)

With Tgrace = 24 mo, kg = 0.12 mo-1, and μfloor = 0.132, Eq. (9) reproduces exactly μsisben = 0.150 (Run 253).

4. Fiscal Erosion & Algedonic Feedback [Eqs. (10)–(14)]
T_loc(t) = psi_tax * GDP_real(t) * Pi(t) * (1 - I_rate * theta_erosion)
dP_inv/dt = tau_eff * T_loc(t) - (G_reac(t) + I_NNAJ(t))
P_mon,rep = SMTH1(P_mon, 1.0 yr) | MPI_rep = SMTH1(MPI_Adj, 1.0 yr)
I_obj(t) = (1 - s_ipm)*100*clamp(1 - P_mon,rep/0.15, 0, 1)
          + s_ipm*100*(1 - MPI_rep)
S_exito = SMTH3(I_obj(t), 1.5 yr)

All fiscal flows are in current COP billions/yr. Lagged SMTH1/SMTH3 states strictly eliminate simultaneous algebraic circularity.

Stage 5 Structural & Sensitivity Verification (Barlas 1996)

1. Multivariate Sobol Sensitivity (N = 200, ±20% across 8 parameters): First-order (S1) and total-order (ST) indices confirm that α0, ηcap, and β0 explain >82% of 2040 informality variance, with 100% ordinal policy ranking invariance (04a ≻ 04b ≻ 03 ≻ 01 ≻ 02).

2. Euler Step-Size Convergence: Halving Δt from 0.25 to 0.125 yr changes 2040 Irate by <0.1 pp and CH by <0.005 CIU.

3. Extreme 1 (INNAJ = 0): Produces an idle fiscal liquidity trap ($11.21B cash hoarded while CH erodes to 0.850 CIU and informality stalls at 43.9%).

4. Extreme 2 (-50% Real GDP): Isolates the endogenous municipal audit contraction channel: as reactive outlays shrink by 49.1%, administrative audit pressure drops, slightly lowering informality (35.3%) even as capabilities erode (1.093 CIU)—illustrating why informality must be read jointly with ICE and MPIAdjusted.
